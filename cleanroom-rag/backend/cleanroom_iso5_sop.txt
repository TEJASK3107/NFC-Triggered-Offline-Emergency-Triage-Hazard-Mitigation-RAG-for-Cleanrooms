ISRO SATELLITE INTEGRATION CLEANROOM STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document ID: SOP-CLN-010
Facility: ISO Class 5 Cleanroom
Substance: Isopropanol (IPA) & Solvents

1. ENTRY & GARBING PROTOCOL
All personnel entering Cleanroom Bay A-1 must execute full garbing procedures:
- Pass through air showers for 30 seconds prior to entry.
- Wear full non-linting coveralls, hood, boot covers, and nitrile cleanroom gloves.
- Mobile phones, outer paper, and non-approved stationery are strictly prohibited inside ISO Class 5 zones.

2. CHEMICAL CLEANING PROCEDURES
- Isopropanol (IPA) used for precision optical wiping must be stored in grounding-rated safety dispensers.
- Limit solvent containers to 500mL capacity per station.
- Dispose of solvent-soaked wipes in closed, fire-resistant hazardous waste bins immediately after use.
